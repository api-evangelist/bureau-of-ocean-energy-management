Preparing for the Future of Offshore Nuclear Energy jules_liu@ios… Wed, 07/22/2026 - 7:08 Release Date 07/23/2026 On July 21, the Marine Minerals Administration (MMA) and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) took an important step toward preparing for the next generation of offshore energy. The two agencies signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) that establishes how they will work together if developers propose nuclear energy projects on the U.S. Outer Continental Shelf in the future.
